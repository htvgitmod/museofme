# Adrian Punk

## Facts
- Creator on X, handle @AdrianPunk115.
- Author of the "Vibe Coding 视觉交互词典" (Vibe Coding Visual Dictionary) series: Part 1 (上篇) has 40 terms plus 1 overall prompt; Part 2 (下篇) has 35 terms plus 1 overall prompt.
- John's Vietnamese-English vibe-coding dictionary draws its visual-dictionary section from Adrian Punk's series, and the file credits him as the author.
- Part 1 stood at #25 on the X Top-100 with 1,654 saves; Part 2 stood at #96 with 574 saves.
- The full text of both parts was found on a mirror site (yuansaysai.com), which notes it is a re-arrangement of public notes rather than the author's own page; the original X post for Part 2 could not be opened, so whether the Part 2 mirror text is verbatim or condensed is unverified.

## History
- 20 November 2026: John sent 61 Xiaohongshu images for the vibe-coding dictionary; the visual-dictionary series among them was identified as Adrian Punk's work and verified live against the mirror, matching all 40 terms plus the overall prompt, including Footer (item 20) and Back to Top (item 30).
- 20 November 2026: John asked for the search for the Part 2 text to continue; the full text was found with 35 terms plus 1 overall prompt, and John directed that all 35 be added to the dictionary.
- 20 November 2026: the dictionary was rebuilt to 982 cards (980 terms plus 2 overall prompts), with Adrian Punk credited in the file and the wrongly applied "supplementary" flags removed from Footer and Back to Top, both of which are his original items.

## The relationship
- Not a personal acquaintance in the evidence; a creator whose published series is the source material for John's dictionary project.
- The tie is one of source and credit, not contact: John's dictionary builds on Adrian Punk's work and names him in it.

## In common
- Vibe-coding terminology and how to describe visual and interaction effects.
- Dictionary and glossary formats for looking up terms and prompts.
