# Ngô Sĩ Liên

## Facts
- Author of the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, the official history of the Lê dynasty, written as annals year by year from Hồng Bàng to 1675.
- The full text of the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư is available on Vietnamese Wikisource.
- On 14 November 2026 HTV chose the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư as one of four source books for making the Vietnam-history timeline dense and fully covered, after finding the 73-milestone build too sparse for how dense Vietnamese history is. The other three are Trần Trọng Kim's Việt Nam sử lược, the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục, and a modern history textbook (SGK) for 1945 to the present.
- The book was assigned as a source for the Cổ đại–Lý period (with Việt Nam sử lược) and the Trần–Tây Sơn period (with the Cương mục and Việt Nam sử lược) in the four-agent dense extraction HTV commissioned on 14 November 2026.
- The merged dense dataset from that extraction has 467 milestones across 399 different years, from the Hồng Bàng legend (−2879) to the 2025 provincial mergers, saved in workspace/side-projects/timeline-su-viet-day-du.md. The timeline will show all 467 milestones; the Trước Hay Sau game keeps one milestone per year (307 starred years).
- On 14 November 2026 the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset, listing every milestone in years with several — 1945 has 7 milestones, and 1930 and 1940 have 3 each — while the game keeps one starred milestone per year (307).
- A few passages were not yet directly cross-checked when the dataset was merged on 14 November 2026, including Toàn thư Quyển 4; those are noted in appendix 3 of the merged dataset file.
- On 15 November 2026 HTV approved fixing all three small display issues in the 467-milestone timeline, and the fixes were completed the same day: 10 milestone titles were cleaned of note suffixes such as "(sự kiện thứ 2 cùng năm)"; the extra stars were removed so 1516 keeps only the Trần Cao loạn starred and 1945 only the Declaration of Independence, bringing the game back to 307 starred years, one per year; and 69 stray "###" markers were cut from notes and sources. The totals did not change: 467 milestones, 1945 still 7.
- On 15 November 2026 the milestone database was uploaded to GitHub (commit f27fe07) as 8 files in source/timeline-su-viet/ on the repo htvgitmod/museofme: a README, four period files (Cổ đại–Lý 100, Trần–Tây Sơn 157, Nguyễn–1945 72, 1945–nay 138), an appendix, the full merged file, and the deep-research file. The interactive timeline page itself is still only a draft and has not been uploaded to GitHub.
- On 19 November 2026 HTV asked for detailed event files written from the history books, not only the milestone summaries: currently only 83 items have the detailed form (Context / Main developments / Significance / People and places / Sources), and about 384 milestones have only the short summary form. The assistant proposed three scope options — detailed files for all 467 milestones (about 1.5 MB total, split by period over several batches), for the 307 starred milestones, or a 20-milestone sample for HTV to approve the format first — and the 20-milestone sample was delivered later the same day (see below).
- On 19 November 2026 the assistant delivered the 20-milestone sample of detailed event files (5,529 words, five per period A–D, all starred milestones in the game, from 187 Sĩ Nhiếp to 2003 SARS), in the same format as the 83 detailed items, with all dates, figures, and book-chapter citations drawn from the dense 467-milestone dataset and the four source books. Two entries were flagged as thin — 1272 Lê Văn Hưu and 1300 Trần Hưng Đạo, where the dense dataset had only one or two lines, marked as needing more research before publishing. On 20 November 2026 the assistant corrected its own claim about the sample: the 1940 entry on Japan entering Indochina (P15) does not fill a gap, because the existing 83 detailed items already contained 1940 as C16a (Japan) plus C16b (Nam Kỳ uprising), so P15 duplicates C16a and was dropped when merging; the unique detailed count before this round was 102, not 103. The sample is local only: it has not been merged into the 83 detailed items and is not on GitHub, pending HTV's approval of the format.
- On 20 November 2026 HTV chose detailed files for all 467 milestones, and six agents wrote the remaining detailed items in parallel, in the format of the 20-milestone sample, drawing figures only from the dense dataset and the four source books and marking thin entries "cần tra thêm".
- The Cổ đại–Lý batch (part A) delivered 76 new detailed items on 20 November 2026. Its agent read Toàn thư Quyển 4 directly on Wikisource (the Tiền Lý and Triệu Việt Vương passages), giving new detail for 542–571 and recording both year readings where the books differ — Lý Bí's uprising 541 in the Toàn thư against 542 in the SGK, and Triệu Quang Phục retaking Long Biên 550 in the Toàn thư against 548 in some books. The 1076 milestone was split into two detailed items: A18 on Quốc Tử Giám and the new A88 on the Khâm–Liêm–Ung campaign. Two part-A items were marked "cần tra thêm" because their corresponding Toàn thư passages had not yet been cross-checked directly: 446 Đàn Hòa Chi and 993 Tống phong Lê Đại Hành.
- On 20 November 2026 the six batches were merged and checked: 468 detailed items for all 467 milestones (1076 in two items), with the game stars corrected to 307, one per year. The merged set was uploaded to GitHub the same day (commit 67d9040) as 5 files in source/timeline-su-viet/ on htvgitmod/museofme: four period detail files (A 101 items, B 157, C 72, D 138) plus the merge report. Confidence: 339 items high, 19 medium, 106 (23%) marked "cần tra thêm", concentrated in part B.
- On 21 November 2026 the assistant checked the undecided year label against Wikipedia and recommended 1285 for the second anti-Yuan resistance: the standalone article dates it to 1285 (late January to late May 1285), and textbooks also call it 1285; 1284 is the lunar month (tháng Chạp Giáp Thân) in which the fighting began. The proposal is to label it 1285 and add a note that fighting began in late 1284, pending HTV's approval.
- On 21 November 2026 HTV approved the 1285 label ("Ghi 1285, thêm chú thích bắt đầu cuối 1284"). Appendix 1 was changed to 1285 in both the dense dataset and phu-luc.md, and the B2 detailed item gained the note that fighting began in late 1284 (tháng Chạp Giáp Thân), i.e. late January 1285; the 5 changed files were uploaded to GitHub the same day (commit 515ac49). The game page itself still uses 1284 (GAME_POOL), so it is not yet in sync with the approved label.
