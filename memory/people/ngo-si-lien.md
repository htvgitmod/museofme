# Ngô Sĩ Liên

## Facts
- Author of the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, the official history of the Lê dynasty, written as annals year by year from Hồng Bàng to 1675.
- The full text of the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư is available on Vietnamese Wikisource.
- On 14 November 2026 John chose the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư as one of four source books for making the Vietnam-history timeline dense and fully covered, after finding the 73-milestone build too sparse for how dense Vietnamese history is. The other three are Trần Trọng Kim's Việt Nam sử lược, the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục, and a modern history textbook (SGK) for 1945 to the present.
- The book was assigned as a source for the Cổ đại–Lý period (with Việt Nam sử lược) and the Trần–Tây Sơn period (with the Cương mục and Việt Nam sử lược) in the four-agent dense extraction John commissioned on 14 November 2026.
- The merged dense dataset from that extraction has 467 milestones across 399 different years, from the Hồng Bàng legend (−2879) to the 2025 provincial mergers, saved in workspace/side-projects/timeline-su-viet-day-du.md. The timeline will show all 467 milestones; the Trước Hay Sau game keeps one milestone per year (307 starred years).
- On 14 November 2026 the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset, listing every milestone in years with several — 1945 has 7 milestones, and 1930 and 1940 have 3 each — while the game keeps one starred milestone per year (307).
- A few passages were not yet directly cross-checked when the dataset was merged on 14 November 2026, including Toàn thư Quyển 4; those are noted in appendix 3 of the merged dataset file.

## History
- 14 November 2026: John said the timeline was too sparse and wanted it made dense from authoritative books, covering everything rather than aiming at a fixed milestone count. The assistant proposed the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư as the backbone for antiquity to the medieval period.
- 14 November 2026: John approved the four-book source list including the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, and four agents extracted events by period, reading the books year by year and chapter by chapter.
- 14 November 2026: the four periods were merged into a single dense dataset of 467 milestones / 399 years.
- 14 November 2026: John asked that years with several milestones list them all, and the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset; an independent check the same day confirmed 1945 shows 7 separate milestones and 1930 and 1940 show 3 each, with the game keeping one starred milestone per year.
