# Trần Trọng Kim

## Facts
- Author of Việt Nam sử lược, a retelling of the whole of Vietnamese history in five eras up to the early 20th century, in clear prose.
- The full text of Việt Nam sử lược is available on Vietnamese Wikisource, and the book is out of copyright.
- On 14 November 2026 John chose Việt Nam sử lược as one of four source books for making the Vietnam-history timeline dense and fully covered, after finding the 73-milestone build too sparse for how dense Vietnamese history is. The other three are the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục, and a modern history textbook (SGK) for 1945 to the present.
- The book was assigned as a source or cross-check for three of the four extraction periods John commissioned on 14 November 2026: Cổ đại–Lý (with the Toàn thư), Trần–Tây Sơn (with the Toàn thư and the Cương mục), and Nguyễn–1945 (with the SGK). Việt Nam sử lược Quyển I, Phần III, chapters IV–V (the Lý dynasty) was read directly during the Cổ đại–Lý extraction.
- The merged dense dataset from that extraction has 467 milestones across 399 different years, from the Hồng Bàng legend (−2879) to the 2025 provincial mergers, saved in workspace/side-projects/timeline-su-viet-day-du.md. The timeline will show all 467 milestones; the Trước Hay Sau game keeps one milestone per year (307 starred years).
- On 14 November 2026 the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset, listing every milestone in years with several — 1945 has 7 milestones, and 1930 and 1940 have 3 each — while the game keeps one starred milestone per year (307).
- A few chapters were not yet directly cross-checked when the dataset was merged on 14 November 2026; those are noted in appendix 3 of the merged dataset file.

## History
- 14 November 2026: John said the timeline was too sparse and wanted it made dense from authoritative books, covering everything rather than aiming at a fixed milestone count. The assistant proposed Việt Nam sử lược as the overall framework and cross-check against the Toàn thư.
- 14 November 2026: John approved the four-book source list including Việt Nam sử lược, and four agents extracted events by period, reading the books year by year and chapter by chapter.
- 14 November 2026: the four periods were merged into a single dense dataset of 467 milestones / 399 years.
- 14 November 2026: John asked that years with several milestones list them all, and the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset; an independent check the same day confirmed 1945 shows 7 separate milestones and 1930 and 1940 show 3 each, with the game keeping one starred milestone per year.
