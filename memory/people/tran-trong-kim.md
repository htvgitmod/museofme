# Trần Trọng Kim

## Facts
- Author of Việt Nam sử lược, a retelling of the whole of Vietnamese history in five eras up to the early 20th century, in clear prose.
- The full text of Việt Nam sử lược is available on Vietnamese Wikisource, and the book is out of copyright.
- On 14 November 2026 John chose Việt Nam sử lược as one of four source books for making the Vietnam-history timeline dense and fully covered, after finding the 73-milestone build too sparse for how dense Vietnamese history is. The other three are the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục, and a modern history textbook (SGK) for 1945 to the present.
- The book was assigned as a source or cross-check for three of the four extraction periods John commissioned on 14 November 2026: Cổ đại–Lý (with the Toàn thư), Trần–Tây Sơn (with the Toàn thư and the Cương mục), and Nguyễn–1945 (with the SGK). Việt Nam sử lược Quyển I, Phần III, chapters IV–V (the Lý dynasty) was read directly during the Cổ đại–Lý extraction.
- The merged dense dataset from that extraction has 467 milestones across 399 different years, from the Hồng Bàng legend (−2879) to the 2025 provincial mergers, saved in workspace/side-projects/timeline-su-viet-day-du.md. The timeline will show all 467 milestones; the Trước Hay Sau game keeps one milestone per year (307 starred years).
- On 14 November 2026 the timeline page was reassembled with the full 467-milestone dataset, listing every milestone in years with several — 1945 has 7 milestones, and 1930 and 1940 have 3 each — while the game keeps one starred milestone per year (307).
- A few chapters were not yet directly cross-checked when the dataset was merged on 14 November 2026; those are noted in appendix 3 of the merged dataset file.
- On 15 November 2026 John approved fixing all three small display issues in the 467-milestone timeline, and the fixes were completed the same day: 10 milestone titles were cleaned of note suffixes such as "(sự kiện thứ 2 cùng năm)"; the extra stars were removed so 1516 keeps only the Trần Cao loạn starred and 1945 only the Declaration of Independence, bringing the game back to 307 starred years, one per year; and 69 stray "###" markers were cut from notes and sources. The totals did not change: 467 milestones, 1945 still 7.
- On 15 November 2026 the milestone database was uploaded to GitHub (commit f27fe07) as 8 files in source/timeline-su-viet/ on the repo htvgitmod/museofme: a README, four period files (Cổ đại–Lý 100, Trần–Tây Sơn 157, Nguyễn–1945 72, 1945–nay 138), an appendix, the full merged file, and the deep-research file. The interactive timeline page itself is still only a draft and has not been uploaded to GitHub.
- On 19 November 2026 John asked for detailed event files written from the history books, not only the milestone summaries: currently only 83 items have the detailed form (Context / Main developments / Significance / People and places / Sources), and about 384 milestones have only the short summary form. The assistant proposed three scope options — detailed files for all 467 milestones (about 1.5 MB total, split by period over several batches), for the 307 starred milestones, or a 20-milestone sample for John to approve the format first — and the 20-milestone sample was delivered later the same day (see below).
- On 19 November 2026 the assistant delivered the 20-milestone sample of detailed event files (5,529 words, five per period A–D, all starred milestones in the game, from 187 Sĩ Nhiếp to 2003 SARS), in the same format as the 83 detailed items, with all dates, figures, and book-chapter citations drawn from the dense 467-milestone dataset and the four source books. Two entries were flagged as thin — 1272 Lê Văn Hưu and 1300 Trần Hưng Đạo, where the dense dataset had only one or two lines, marked as needing more research before publishing. On 20 November 2026 the assistant corrected its own claim about the sample: the 1940 entry on Japan entering Indochina (P15) does not fill a gap, because the existing 83 detailed items already contained 1940 as C16a (Japan) plus C16b (Nam Kỳ uprising), so P15 duplicates C16a and was dropped when merging; the unique detailed count before this round was 102, not 103. The sample is local only: it has not been merged into the 83 detailed items and is not on GitHub, pending John's approval of the format.
- On 20 November 2026 John chose detailed files for all 467 milestones, and six agents wrote the remaining detailed items in parallel, in the format of the 20-milestone sample, drawing figures only from the dense dataset and the four source books and marking thin entries "cần tra thêm".
- The Trần–Lê sơ batch (part B1, 1228–1524) delivered 81 new detailed items on 20 November 2026. Its agent read Việt Nam sử lược chapter VI (Trần) and chapter XIV (Lam Sơn) directly on Wikisource to cross-check. Nine items were marked "cần tra thêm"; four of them were Trần-decline battles against Champa (1318, 1367–1368, 1378, 1380) where the dense dataset had only one line. Troop numbers were recorded with their sources, not as absolutes — for example, Tụy Động "giết hơn 5 vạn" is noted as a figure Việt Nam sử lược itself doubts.
- The Mạc–Tây Sơn batch (part B2, 1527–1802) delivered 53 new detailed items on 20 November 2026. Its agent read Việt Nam sử lược across many chapters to cross-check, recording troop numbers with their sources (the Thanh army 20–29 vạn depending on the source, the Xiêm army 2 vạn in Việt Nam sử lược against 5 vạn in another source). Seventeen items were marked "cần tra thêm" at various levels, the most in this round, mainly Mạc milestones and mid-17th-century Nguyễn lords where the dense dataset had only one or two lines.
- On 20 November 2026 the six batches were merged and checked: 468 detailed items for all 467 milestones (1076 in two items), with the game stars corrected to 307, one per year. The merged set was uploaded to GitHub the same day (commit 67d9040) as 5 files in source/timeline-su-viet/ on htvgitmod/museofme: four period detail files (A 101 items, B 157, C 72, D 138) plus the merge report. Confidence: 339 items high, 19 medium, 106 (23%) marked "cần tra thêm", concentrated in part B.
- One label is still undecided after the merge on 20 November 2026: the game year for the second anti-Yuan resistance, 1284 in appendix 1 against 1285 in the dense dataset and the 83-item file; the assistant asked John to choose.
