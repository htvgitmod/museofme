# Lão Tử

## Facts
- Thinker behind the Đạo Đức Kinh, the text John's "Hỏi Lão Tử — 81 quyết định mỗi ngày" / "Thư Tay Lão Tử" project is built on.
- The project was first framed as turning the book's 81 chapters into daily decisions for 22–35-year-olds in Vietnam: pick an emotion or situation, get a chapter and a small action to do in 5 minutes, 24 hours, or 1 week.
- On 13 November 2026 John changed that framing: the page should take Laozi's words straight and add nothing — no invented situations, no modern interpretation, and no small actions (5 minutes / 24 hours / 1 week). The daily-decision framing above is therefore superseded.
- As revised on 13 November 2026, each chapter's letter contains only the chapter number, an excerpted line of the original Chinese text (Wang Bi version), a source line, Lão Tử's signature, and the red seal 老子; the cabinet of 81 chapters lists only each chapter number with its opening Chinese line, and copy/download contain only the original text.
- Each chapter originally carried only one excerpted Chinese line, not the full text of the chapter; on 14 November 2026 John decided the approved format gives each chapter the full Chinese original instead.
- The 81 chapters were grouped into 8 decision clusters during the research and content preparation; the project also had 20 famous lines and 15 situation pieces mapping situations to chapters (being criticised by a boss → chapter 13, FOMO → chapter 46, burnout → chapter 48). Those situation mappings were removed from the page in the 13 November 2026 revisions.
- The original Chinese text is out of copyright; the project's interpretations must be written in John's own words, not copied from modern Vietnamese translations (Nguyễn Duy Cần, Nguyễn Hiến Lê, Phan Ngọc, Vũ Thế Ngọc) or Stephen Mitchell.
- Content for the project was prepared on 13 November 2026: all 81 chapters plus 15 situation pieces, saved under workspace/dao-duc-kinh-content/.
- On 13 November 2026 John chose the project's presentation: a handwritten letter from Lão Tử, one short letter each morning, in ancient style — bamboo slips, old paper, or a cloth scroll — not a modern envelope.
- The demo "Thư Tay Lão Tử" was built on 13 November 2026: a letter on old paper in a scroll with two wooden rollers, 15 bamboo slips by situation plus a random draw, and a cabinet of all 81 chapters with search. Each opened letter started "Gửi bạn…", gave the chapter, 3 small actions (5 minutes / 24 hours / 1 week), a question to reflect on, Lão Tử's signature, and the red seal 老子.
- The demo was tested on 13 November 2026: bamboo slip 7 (Burnout) opened chapter 48 correctly, the page did not overflow on a 390px phone, and there were no console errors. The old-style font fell back to a serif, and the Copy and Download .txt buttons had not yet been tested automatically.
- On 13 November 2026 the demo went live on GitHub Pages at htvgitmod.github.io/museofme/source/thu-tay-lao-tu/thu-tay-lao-tu.html: file `source/thu-tay-lao-tu/thu-tay-lao-tu.html` on the repo `htvgitmod/museofme`, branch main, 331 lines, 998 KB, commit `0cb17be` at 15:55. The live check confirmed the title, scroll and bamboo-slip images, random draw, 15 bamboo slips by situation, the sample letter for chapter 8, and the cabinet of all 81 chapters. The first upload landed in the wrong folder and was moved to the correct place, with no other repo files touched. John had already provided the GitHub account `htvgitmod`, and the saved login was used.
- On 13 November 2026 John told the assistant not to add his personal details or images to the page, and to keep it generic about Lão Tử.
- The demo was revised on 13 November 2026 in response: the 15 bamboo slips written from personal life situations (being criticised by a boss, side project, salary negotiation, being called flop) and the "Gửi bạn" letter narrating the reader's own story were removed, and replaced with 8 bamboo slips following the book's 8 major themes (Nhìn cho đúng, Biết đủ biết dừng, Vô vi, Mềm như nước…). The letter then presented only generic chapter content: the Chinese original, when to use it, an interpretation, a small suggested action, and a question to reflect on. The introduction was changed to a general introduction to the Đạo Đức Kinh, and search, random draw, copy and download still worked.
- The demo's hero image is a generic illustration (scroll, bamboo slips, brush), with no image of John in it, so it was kept in the revision.
- On 13 November 2026, in the evening, John objected to an invented situation in chapter 1 ("Khi cả team cãi nhau nửa buổi chỉ vì một từ…") and said to take Laozi's words straight and add nothing at all, including the small actions. The demo was revised again the same evening: the "dùng khi nào" sections with invented situations, the modern-language interpretations, the small actions (5 minutes / 24 hours / 1 week), the reflection questions, and the 8 theme slips were all removed. The word "flop" in chapter 58 went with the removed interpretation.
- On 13 November 2026 the "Kho Tổng" hub page added a card for "Thư Tay Lão Tử", placed after the Track 5-3 link and before the old archives, linking to the 81-chapter letter page.
- On 14 November 2026 John clarified the 13 November verbatim-only rule: the page must have Vietnamese and a general explanation (drawn from other parties' general explanations of each chapter), because Chinese alone is not usable for the reader. His own opinion or additions are not wrong and are encouraged, as long as they rest on the general explanation and avoid controversial views or targeting any specific person. Fabricated specific situations (team arguing, a boss criticising) and John's personal details are still not to be included.
- On 14 November 2026 the demo was revised to that clarification: each chapter now has a Vietnamese title, a cluster name, the original Chinese text, and a "Giải nghĩa bằng tiếng Việt" section taking only the "Ý chính" from the prepared content — not "Dùng khi nào" and not "Hôm nay làm gì". Narrow transferred meanings such as "sếp, bảng xếp hạng trong team" in chapters 3, 10 and 17 were cut, chapters 57 and 60 were rephrased in general terms, and parenthetical transferred-meaning notes were removed from chapters 30, 31, 61, 68, 69, 72, 74, 75, 77 and 78. The cabinet of 81 bamboo slips now shows a Vietnamese title on each slip and is searchable in Vietnamese, and copy/download now include the title and the explanation. The small actions (5 minutes / 24 hours / 1 week) were still not brought back.
- On 14 November 2026 John approved the deep-explanation format, using chapter 8 ("Sống như nước") as the model: each chapter has exactly three parts in order — (1) the full original Chinese text (Wang Bi version), (2) "Giải nghĩa", a full Vietnamese translation in the assistant's own words, and (3) the deep part in three layers or points (for chapter 8: water benefits without contending; water dwells in the lowest place; the seven "thiện") — with no concluding sentence.
- On 14 November 2026 the deep explanations for all 81 chapters in that format were completed: 81/81 chapters, counted again by script, each chapter's Chinese original cross-checked against at least two sources, no chapter left unverified, and variant readings between printed editions noted in the files (workspace/dao-duc-kinh-content/deep/, about 244 KB). Chapter 57 was renamed "Càng ít luật, càng tự chạy", dropping the word "team".
- On 14 November 2026 John then clarified what part 2 of each chapter should be, in three steps: first he asked for a translation of each Chinese character into Vietnamese, then he corrected that to a plain Vietnamese translation of the sentence with no explanation (his example: "where are you from" translates plainly as "bạn đến từ đâu", while explaining it is a separate step), and then he corrected that again to Hán-Việt — the Sino-Vietnamese reading of each Chinese character, with no per-character explanation and not a modern prose translation. His chapter 8 example reads: "Thượng thiện nhược thủy. Thủy thiện lợi vạn vật nhi bất tranh, xứ chúng nhân chi sở ố, cố cơ ư Đạo. Cư thiện địa, tâm thiện uyên, dữ thiện nhân, ngôn thiện tín, chính thiện trị, sự thiện năng, động thiện thời. Phù duy bất tranh, cố vô vưu."
- On 14 November 2026 John approved the final format: each chapter has (1) the full original Chinese text, (2) Hán-Việt transliteration, and (3) "Giải nghĩa", keeping the old prose Vietnamese translation merged together with the three-layer deep part in that one section. The Hán-Việt for all 81 chapters was written the same day, in 8 parallel groups, and counted at 81 lines.
- The two Hán-Việt readings that were undecided on 14 November 2026 are now settled: chapter 5, 數 in "Đa ngôn 數窮", is read "sác" (the adverb shuò, "speaking much soon runs out"), and chapter 41, 纇 in "夷道若纇", is read "lỗi" (a crack or flaw). John said on 14 November 2026 that he had no experience to choose between the readings and left the decision to the assistant; the deep files were corrected to those readings, with no chapters left flagged for checking and 81/81 Hán-Việt lines verified.
- On 14 November 2026 the page was revised to the final format: all 81 chapters in the three parts (full Chinese original, Hán-Việt, "Giải nghĩa"), searchable in the Hán-Việt text as well, copy/download including all three parts, and the old trailing " ---" error fixed in 69 chapters. The small actions (5 minutes / 24 hours / 1 week) were still not brought back.
- On 14 November 2026 at 13:57 the final version was uploaded to GitHub: the old file was deleted (commit `7be5f57`) and the new file uploaded (commit `6578e55`, 303 lines, 1.09 MB) to `source/thu-tay-lao-tu/thu-tay-lao-tu.html` on `htvgitmod/museofme`, using the saved `htvgitmod` login. The live check confirmed all three parts per chapter, chapter 8 opening by default, chapter 5 reading "Đa ngôn sác cùng, bất như thủ trung", chapter 41 reading "di đạo nhược lỗi", and the 15 old situation cards gone. A leftover CSS definition for `.slips` / `.slip` remains in the new file's source but displays nothing on the page.
